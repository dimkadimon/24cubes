Martin Gardner's cube-touching puzzle

Face contact only — the 24-cube construction found by Robert S. Holmes, reported in Wheels, Life and Other Mathematical Amusements (1983).

How the 24 cubes are distributed

Tally: two faces get 7 each, one gets 6, the opposite one gets 2, and the last two get 1 each: 7+7+6+2+1+1 = 24. This is a construction, not a proof that 24 is optimal.
